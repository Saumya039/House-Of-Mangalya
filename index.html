Our Heritage

From Sacred Earth
to Your Home

"In our culture, turmeric is not just a spice — it is a blessing, a remedy, and a ritual passed down through generations."

House of Mangalya was born from a deep reverence for India's most sacred golden root. Mangalya — meaning auspicious and blessed — captures the spirit of turmeric's role in Indian life, from marriage ceremonies and healing rituals to everyday kitchens.


We work hand-in-hand with third-generation farmers who use time-honoured cultivation methods alongside sustainable organic practices. From the moment the rhizome is planted to the careful sun-drying and stone-grinding process, every step honours both the land and the tradition.
