🌿 Product & Quality

Our current batch contains 5.5% curcumin content (curcuminoids), verified by an ISO 17025 accredited third-party laboratory. This is significantly higher than the market average of 1.5–2%, which is why our turmeric is deeper in colour, richer in aroma, and more potent in its wellness effects. Curcumin percentage can vary slightly between harvests (5.0–5.8%) — we publish the exact figure for every batch on our lab report, available on request.
When stored correctly, our turmeric powder has a shelf life of 24 months from the date of manufacturing (printed on the bottom of the jar). For best potency and aroma, we recommend consuming within 12 months of opening. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Our airtight glass jar with a foam-sealed lid is specifically designed to maximise freshness and protect the volatile oils responsible for turmeric's characteristic aroma.
Absolutely not. Our turmeric is 100% pure Curcuma longa rhizome powder — nothing more, nothing less. It contains zero artificial colour (including lead chromate), zero starch extenders (rice flour, maida), zero preservatives, and zero anti-caking agents. This is verified in every batch lab report. Unfortunately, adulteration is rampant in the Indian turmeric market — we built House of Mangalya specifically to change that.
We use the Erode Salem variety (Curcuma longa), cultivated in Erode, Tamil Nadu — the largest turmeric trading district in the world, known for producing exceptionally high curcumin varieties. The red laterite soil, specific microclimate, and traditional cultivation methods of this region yield turmeric with naturally superior curcumin levels compared to varieties grown elsewhere.
Our turmeric is processed using traditional cold stone-grinding on granite mills. This low-speed, low-heat method is critical — high-speed industrial grinding generates significant heat that degrades volatile oils, curcuminoids, and the natural complexity of the rhizome. Our stone-grinding process takes longer but preserves the full spectrum of bioactive compounds that make our turmeric genuinely potent.

🔬 Certifications & Lab Testing

House of Mangalya holds the following certifications:
  • USDA Organic (NOP) — Certificate #NOP-2024-HOM. Confirms zero synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or chemical fertilisers.
  • India Organic (NPOP) — National Programme for Organic Production compliance. Covers our entire supply chain from farm to packaged product.
  • ISO 17025 Lab Reports — Batch-specific, third-party accredited testing for curcumin content, heavy metals, pesticide residues, and microbial load.
All certificates and batch lab reports are available to customers on request via email at certs@houseofmangalya.com.
Yes. Every batch is tested for heavy metals (lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury) and 400+ pesticide residues by an independent, ISO 17025 accredited laboratory before shipping. Results consistently show heavy metals and pesticide residues below detectable limits (BDL). We specifically test for lead chromate — the adulterant most commonly used to artificially boost the visual colour of turmeric — and our product consistently returns zero.
Absolutely. We operate with full transparency. Email us at certs@houseofmangalya.com with your order number and the batch number printed on your jar. We will send you the complete third-party lab report within 24 hours at no charge. We believe every customer has the right to know exactly what is in their food.
