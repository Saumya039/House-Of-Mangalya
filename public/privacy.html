Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to deliver your order, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, email address, mobile number and delivery address, the items you bought, and whether your payment succeeded. If you join our newsletter we collect your email address.

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled entirely inside the secure window of our payment partner, Razorpay.

To understand which of our channels brings visitors, we note — anonymously and without identifying you — the website or campaign link you arrived from. Your shopping cart is kept in your own browser (local storage) and is not sent to us until you check out.

Why we use it

To process and deliver your order, send you updates about it, provide support and returns, issue GST invoices, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations (tax and accounting records). If you subscribe, we send you our newsletter and offers; you can unsubscribe at any time. We rely on your consent and on the need to fulfil your order.

Who we share it with

Only the partners needed to serve you: Razorpay (payments), courier partners such as Delhivery, BlueDart and India Post (they receive your name, address and phone to deliver), and our hosting and email providers. They may use your data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally several years). Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Marketing data is deleted on request.

How we protect it

Orders are stored on access-controlled systems, administrative access is password-protected and logged, connections to the site are encrypted, and we collect no more data than we need.

Your rights

You may ask us to show you the personal data we hold, correct it, erase it (subject to records we must legally keep), withdraw your consent, or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Email hello@houseofmangalya.com — we respond within 7 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

Consent and notice

We ask for your agreement (a tick-box at checkout, or the newsletter form) before we collect your data, and we tell you at that point what it is used for. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to the Grievance Officer below; withdrawing is as easy as giving it, but we may still have to keep order and tax records the law requires. Newsletter emails carry an unsubscribe option.

Where your data is stored

Order data is held in an access-controlled, encrypted database hosted in Singapore (Neon, on Amazon Web Services) and served through Vercel. Payments are processed by Razorpay in India. We take reasonable security safeguards to protect your data and, if a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what the site needs: your cart and coupon are kept in your browser’s local storage, and a short-lived security cookie is used only for our staff sign-in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Grievance Officer

As required by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, complaints about an order, a product, this website or your personal data can be sent to our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month.
