Delivery & Returns

Shipping & Returns

Free standard shipping above ₹999 and a 14-day return window.

Delivery within India

  • Standard (5–7 business days): ₹80, free on orders above ₹999 after any coupon discount.
  • Express (2–3 business days): ₹150.
  • Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad) are typically 2–4 days on standard delivery.

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation, through partners such as Delhivery, BlueDart and India Post. International orders are not available at checkout yet — please email hello@houseofmangalya.com if you would like to order from outside India.

Tracking your order

Once your order ships we share the courier name and tracking number. You can always check progress on our Track Your Order page using your order number and the email you ordered with.

Packaging

Turmeric is packed in an airtight glass jar with a tamper-evident lid, inside a protective box with biodegradable cushioning.

Damaged or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please email photographs to returns@houseofmangalya.com within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund.

Returns & refunds

You may return an unopened or defective product within 14 days of delivery in its original packaging. To start a return email returns@houseofmangalya.com with your order number and reason; we reply within 24 hours with a pickup label. Once the product reaches us and passes inspection, your refund is processed within 3–5 business days to the original payment method (your bank may take a few more days to show it).

Cancellations

You can cancel before your order is packed by emailing us; we will refund in full. Once shipped, please use the returns process above.

Grievance Officer

As required by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, complaints about an order, a product, this website or your personal data can be sent to our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month.
